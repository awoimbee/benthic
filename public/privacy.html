benthic Local-first dive log

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The short version: benthic is a local-first application. It has no server and no account. Your dive log stays on your device. It only leaves your device if you explicitly turn on remote sync and connect a storage service that you own and control. The developer never receives your data.

Who we are

benthic is an open-source project hosted at github.com/awoimbee/benthic. This policy explains what the application does with your information.

Data stored on your device

By default, benthic stores everything locally, on the device you use:

  • your dive log (dives, sites, trips, equipment, notes and, where present, GPS coordinates) and any imported files;
  • your display preferences and saved filter presets;
  • a rolling automatic backup of your log, kept so you can recover from an accidental change; and
  • per-device dive-computer download bookkeeping (which dives have already been downloaded).

On the web this lives in your browser's site storage (IndexedDB with a localStorage fallback). On the desktop app it lives in the operating system's application-data directory. This data is not transmitted to the developer and is not accessible to us.

Optional remote sync

benthic can optionally sync your log through a storage service that you configure yourself. This is off by default. When you enable it, the application talks directly from your device to the service you chose — there is no intermediary server operated by benthic. The remote copy is stored in your own account, and you can delete it at any time from that service.

The available backends today are:

  • Google Drive. The log is stored in the application's private app-data folder (appDataFolder), using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This scope only exposes files that benthic itself creates; benthic cannot see, read or modify any other file in your Google Drive.
  • A Git repository (for example GitHub or a compatible host). The log is stored as a single file in a repository you specify, using a personal access token that you provide.

Credentials needed for sync (an OAuth access/refresh token or a personal access token) are stored locally alongside your sync settings, in the same on-device storage described above. They are used only to authenticate your device to the service you configured. You can sign out from a service at any time, which removes the stored credentials.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, benthic accesses only the contents of its own private app-data folder in order to store and retrieve your dive log. It does not access other Google user data. Google user data is used solely to provide the sync feature you requested, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is never transferred to the developer.

benthic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a server and we never receive your dive log.
  • We do not require or create user accounts.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking pixels or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

The map view requests map tiles directly from the tile providers (Esri for satellite imagery and OpenStreetMap for the street layer); those requests are subject to the providers' own policies, and your dive data is not sent with them.

Third-party services

When you choose to use remote sync or the map, your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub: GitHub privacy statement
  • OpenStreetMap: OSMF privacy policy

Retention and deletion

  • Local data: clear your browser's site data for benthic (or delete the application's data directory on desktop) to remove everything. You can also export your log to a file first if you want a copy.
  • Remote data: delete the file from the connected service (for example, remove benthic.json from the Git repository, or delete the app's data from your Google account), and sign out from within benthic to remove the stored credentials.
  • Google access: you can revoke benthic's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Children

benthic is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page, and the page's history is public in the project repository.
